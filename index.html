What HMR claims

In HMR, space, time, and matter are not the bottom layer of reality. Universal Intelligence (UI) is. Read that way, the open problems of physics change shape.

The physics page sets those problems side by side, then makes a serious attempt at the hardest one, gravity. The attempt is not finished.

Every label below says whose claim it is. Established science carries a source. HMR's view is HMR's. Open means nobody has the answer yet.

Open problems, reframed

Each card is a question physics has not settled, with how HMR reads it. These are HMR's readings, not results.

HMR's view

The Big Bang

Physics today: the universe expanded from a hot, dense state 13.8 billion years ago. Before the first 10⁻⁴³ seconds the theories fail, and why it happened at all is not addressed.

HMR: the moment UI's first complete model of itself closed and ignited. A thought exploding into a greater thought, not matter appearing from nothing.

HMR conjecture Open

Inflation

Physics today: a brief burst of exponential expansion fits the observations well, but the field that drove it is hypothetical.

HMR conjectures that inflation is growth after the first complete model closed. One rule of growth gives steady exponential growth in computer runs. Version 2 found that what grows in that rule has no dimension, so no rule stands behind the conjecture yet.

HMR conjecture In progress

Gravity

Physics today: mass and energy curve spacetime, and gravity has no quantum description.

HMR: reality's trend toward coherence, written out, leads to Einstein's equations, with three steps still open. Version 2 adds a conjecture that gravity is the large-scale behavior of the order of thoughts, not a field to be quantized. Which of two forms it takes is open.

Established science HMR's view

Dark energy

Physics today: about 68% of the universe, a placeholder for whatever makes expansion speed up. The predicted vacuum energy misses by about 10¹²⁰.

HMR: not a substance, but the continuing work of the universe modeling itself. If space-time is a count of N thoughts, the count wanders by about its square root, so Λ is about 1/√N. With one thought per Planck volume that is 4 × 10⁻¹²², and the measured value is 2.8 × 10⁻¹²².

The formula is causal set theory's, not HMR's: Ahmed, Dodelson, Greene, and Sorkin, 2004.

Open

Dark matter

Physics today: galaxies move as if they held far more mass than we can see, and no dark matter particle has been found.

HMR has no worked-out answer. In HMR gravity belongs to the pattern of distance, so it need not be a particle, but until the mathematics reproduces how a galaxy turns, this is a hypothesis without a mechanism.

HMR conjecture

Empty space

Physics today: quantum theory fills the vacuum with jittering fields whose energy should be enormous, and measurement says it is tiny.

HMR: empty space is not a sea of things. Only structure in the relations carries weight, and evenly spread energy drops out of HMR's route to gravity.

HMR's view Open

Measurement

Physics today: quantum systems hold many possibilities until measured, and what a measurement is remains disputed after a century.

HMR: a measurement is an instance of UI making one possibility definite in its own experience. In Version 2 it is a commit, the making of a record. No rule yet says when one happens.

Established science HMR's view

Entanglement

Physics today: two particles far apart stay correlated in ways no signal could carry.

HMR: distance was mechanized by UI and is not fundamental, so things joined in UI's pattern stay joined however far apart they are placed.

Established: the joining can be handed on, and particles that never met become linked when their partners are measured together (Jennewein and others, 2002).

HMR's view

The speed of light

Physics today: a universal maximum speed, taken as a postulate.

HMR: a change can reach only what is built on it. That order of building is the light cone, the same for every observer. The speed of light is the exchange rate between a step of time and a unit of distance. The physics page labels this established for any pattern shaped like spacetime.

Established science HMR's view

The Planck scale

Physics today: below about 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m known physics loses meaning. Nobody has measured this length. It is worked out from three measured constants.

HMR: the first distance, the unit of one, is the Planck length, to within a factor of a few. It is set by one thought per unit of volume, so the unit is a volume first and a length second.

Established: a grain of volume, unlike a grain of length, looks the same to observers moving at any speed (Bombelli, Henson, and Sorkin, 2009).

HMR's view In progress

The arrow of time

Physics today: the laws run equally well backward, and time's direction is traced to the low entropy of the beginning.

HMR: time is the sequence of UI's thought, and it runs one way because maturation does. Version 2 proves that the number of possible histories of a pattern never falls. That is not yet the second law for matter.

HMR's view

Consciousness

Physics today: not part of physics. Why a physical process is felt from the inside is called the hard problem.

HMR: fundamental. Physics describes the stable rules inside an experience, and it was never going to find experience inside its rules.

The road to quantum mechanics

Quantum theory sits exactly one rung above ordinary chance. HMR's rule of growth climbs that rung when its numbers are allowed to be complex. Here is how much of that is established, and how much is not.

Established science

Ordinary chance and the rung above

Ordinary chance obeys one rule: the chance of this or that is the sum of the two. Quantum theory breaks that rule for pairs and keeps it for triples. Two paths interfere, and three paths add nothing new (Sorkin, 1994).

Measured with light through three slits: three-path interference is under one hundredth of two-path (Sinha and others, 2010).

Established science HMR's view

Linked outcomes, up to nature's limit

On the standard test of linked outcomes, ordinary chance scores at most 2. A rule one rung up, whose weights stay positive in the strong sense, scores at most 2√2, about 2.83, the limit of quantum theory. Without that condition the score reaches 4, which nature does not reach (Craig and others, 2007).

HMR reads this as a condition UI's rule carries.

HMR's view Open

HMR's toy rule

With complex strength, pairs of histories interfere and triples do not, for every pattern the task force has counted. This rule is a toy: it is not well defined at large size.

A quantum rule for births, the test inside a grown pattern, and a rule that grows three dimensions of space are all still in progress or open in science.

Thoughts in an order, with no meaning to the birth order of unrelated ones, is the structure physicists call a causal set. Counting thoughts gives volume, and the longest chain gives time.

The cosmological constant formula, entanglement that can be handed on, and the grain of volume above, each with the paper linked on its card.

The three-path and linked-outcome results of the quantum road.

Where HMR falls short

How a thought comes to be about another is still open. The definitions are proved for single thoughts, not for human minds.

No rule yet grows space, holds a memory in one place, or makes a particle.

HMR predicts no number that physics lacks, and has made no progress on any famous open problem.

Physics of the body

The physics page links to a physical health model, which reads the body as a physical system. The reference it measures against is the perfect body, an optimized tensegrity defined by physics. That reference is HMR's proposal, to be tested by simulation.

Established science

What science knows about body maps

The cortex holds maps of the body, found by stimulating the brain in surgery (Penfield and Boldrey, 1937).

Some children born without a limb still feel it, which suggests part of the body map is inborn (Melzack, 1990).

Hypothesis

What HMR proposes

Beneath the learned maps, the brain holds a subconscious, ideal map of the body: the optimal tensegrity, given by UI. Alongside it is a learned map of the body as it actually is.

This is a hypothesis to test, not an established finding.
